Privacy Policy — Unbolt!

Last updated: 2026-07-28

Unbolt! ("the app", "we") is a single-player puzzle game. This policy explains what data the app and its service providers handle.

Data we store on your device

Your game progress is saved locally on your device only:

  • Level progress, stars, and unlocked levels
  • Coins, piggy-bank total, boosters, and owned themes
  • Daily-challenge streak
  • Sound and haptics settings

We do not collect this data ourselves, and it is removed if you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Unbolt! displays ads through Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google’s SDK may collect and process:

  • Your device’s advertising identifier (Advertising ID)
  • Device and app information, approximate location (derived from IP), and ad-interaction data

This data is collected and used by Google as an independent controller in accordance with its own policies. Learn more and manage choices:

  • How Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • AdMob & privacy: support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543
  • You can reset or opt out of ad personalization in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

Network

The app connects to the internet only to load ads. It has no accounts and no login.

What we do NOT do

  • We do not collect your name, email, contacts, photos, or precise GPS location.
  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • We do not use third-party analytics beyond what AdMob requires to serve ads.

Children

Unbolt! is intended for a general audience. Because it shows ads, we do not knowingly direct it at children under 13, and ad content is served under Google’s families/policy controls where applicable.

Your choices

You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads at any time in your device’s system settings. Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data.

Changes

We may update this policy; material changes will be reflected here before they take effect.
