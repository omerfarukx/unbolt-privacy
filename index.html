Privacy Policy — Unbolt!

Last updated: 2026-07-11

Unbolt! is a single-player, fully offline puzzle game built to respect your privacy completely.

The short version

Unbolt! does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. There are no ads, no analytics, no accounts, and no network requests. Everything the app stores stays on your device.

What the app stores (on your device only)

  • Level progress, stars, and unlocked levels
  • Coins, piggy-bank total, boosters, and owned themes
  • Daily-challenge streak
  • Sound and haptics settings

This data never leaves your device and is removed if you uninstall the app or clear its data.

What we do NOT do

  • No personal information collected (no name, email, location, contacts, or device identifiers).
  • No advertising or analytics SDKs.
  • No network connections — the app requests no INTERNET permission.
  • No sharing or selling of data, because we don't have any.

Children

Unbolt! is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If a future update changes data handling (for example, optional ads or cloud save), this policy will be updated before that update ships.
